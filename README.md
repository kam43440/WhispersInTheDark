Whispers in the Dark

Whispers in the Dark is a two-player co-op psychological horror game developed in Unreal Engine 5.7.

Players explore an abandoned psychiatric hospital that exists inside the fragmented memories of a patient undergoing experimental therapy. The story is discovered through exploration, environmental storytelling, communication, and cooperative puzzles.

Current features

- Steam and LAN multiplayer sessions
- Steam friend list, invitations, and joining
- Two-player listen-server support
- Push-to-talk proximity voice chat
- Replicated flashlight
- Server-validated interaction system
- Multiplayer level travel
- Main menu and pause menu
- Persistent video and audio settings
- Configurable interactive actors and travel doors

Technology

- Unreal Engine 5.7
- Blueprints and C++
- Enhanced Input
- Online Subsystem Steam
- Steam Sockets
- Advanced Sessions Plugin
- Unreal VOIP
- UMG and SaveGame

Planned systems

- First-person gameplay
- Inventory and item pickups
- Keys and locked doors
- Flashlight battery
- Phone communication
- Mask and headphones mechanics
- Cooperative puzzles
- Hallucinations and psychological stability
- Enemy AI
- Playable hospital vertical slice

## Status

The project is currently a technical prototype. Multiplayer, voice chat, menus, settings, interactions, and level travel are functional, while the main gameplay systems are still in development.
